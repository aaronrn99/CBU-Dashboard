    Dropbox

    Connect Dropbox to browse, preview, and open your architecture files directly from the Files tab.

    How to get your access token
    1. Go to dropbox.com/developers/apps
    2. Click Create App
    3. Choose Scoped access → Full Dropbox
    4. Name the app and click Create App
    5. On the app page, open the Settings tab and scroll to OAuth 2
    6. Under Generated access token, click Generate
    7. Copy the token and paste it above

    Your token is stored only in this browser's localStorage and is sent only to Dropbox's servers.
